Why Self-Hosted Matters for Legal Aid

Your clients trust you with sensitive information. The software you use should respect that.

Client Data Stays Local

RedRope runs on your machine. No data is sent to external servers. No cloud dependency. Attorney-client privilege protected at the infrastructure level.

No Ongoing Cost

Self-hosted means no monthly subscription. For organizations running on grants and donations, that matters. One install, use it forever.

No Vendor Lock-In

You own the installation. Export your data anytime. If you outgrow us, take your data and go. No hostage situations.

Remote Access Built In

Self-hosted doesn't mean chained to one machine. Built-in secure tunnel lets you access RedRope from courthouse, home, or mobile device.
