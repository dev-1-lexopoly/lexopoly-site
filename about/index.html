Why We Exist

Most practice management software is too expensive for solo attorneys and small firms. Per-seat pricing, mandatory cloud hosting, and locked-in ecosystems add up fast. Meanwhile, AI tools like ChatGPT have no privacy protections for client data.

Lexopoly builds the alternative: affordable practice management with a self-hosted option, private AI that keeps data on your network, and Clio add-on tools that work with your existing setup.

How We're Different

  • Self-Hosted Option: RedRope runs on your own server. Client data never touches our infrastructure. Attorney-client privilege protected by architecture.
  • Private AI: FirmMind's patent-pending technology keeps your data on your network while providing AI-powered drafting and analysis via Claude, GPT, and more.
  • Affordable: RedRope from $20. No per-seat fees eating into a solo practice. Transparent pricing across every product.
  • Works With Clio Too: RevenueRescue, CASS Calculator, and QuoteCreator integrate with Clio. Use them alongside Clio, or switch to RedRope.
