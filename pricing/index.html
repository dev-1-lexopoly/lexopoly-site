Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in RedRope?
RedRope is a complete practice management platform: matters, contacts, billing, trust accounting, documents, calendar, and API access. Available self-hosted or as a managed cloud deployment.
How is FirmMind different from ChatGPT?
FirmMind uses patent-pending privacy architecture that keeps your data on your network. Unlike ChatGPT, your conversations and documents are never used for training and never leave your control. Built specifically for legal professionals who need AI without compromising attorney-client privilege.
Can I use Lexopoly products with Clio?
Yes. RevenueRescue, CASS Calculator, and QuoteCreator are Clio add-ons that integrate directly with your Clio account. Alternatively, you can use RedRope as a standalone practice management platform.

Contact us for product demos. All products include a 30-day money-back guarantee, so you can try any product risk-free.
